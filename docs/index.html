WORDS, WITH EXPRESSION

Let the delivery come through.

Experimental speech cues assess emotional delivery, monotone speech, emphasis, pacing, laughter and sighs from an audio excerpt. Choose your model and cue categories, correct its interpretations, and copy richer context for an AI conversation.

Off by default. Audio analysis is an explicitly requested, paid cloud call on retained recordings. Interpretations can be wrong; plain dictation stays unchanged.

CLEAR ABOUT YOUR DATA

Local history.
Explicit choices.

History and recovery files stay on your device. Cloud engines receive audio when you use them. Local speech uses your configured local server.

Successful-take retention is opt-in. Recovery audio is on by default and expires. Audio and transcripts are not encrypted by the app; API keys use OS encryption.
