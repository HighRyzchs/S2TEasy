YOUR WORDS MEAN SOMETHING

A vocabulary
you can actually edit.

Names. Places. The expressions only your team uses. Add explicit corrections, see every rule, and turn them off whenever you like.

Want to explore pronunciation learning? Opt in to local audio retention, record a practice take without typing into other apps, and export the audio with its intended text. You own those examples.

Vocabulary corrections and prompt hints are implemented. Acoustic fine-tuning, automatic accent learning, and word-level alignment are future work.

CLEAR ABOUT YOUR DATA

Local history.
Explicit choices.

History and recovery files stay on your device. Cloud engines receive audio when you use them. Local speech uses your configured local server.

Successful-take retention is opt-in. Recovery audio is on by default and expires. Audio and transcripts are not encrypted by the app; API keys use OS encryption.
